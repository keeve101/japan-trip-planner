## Deliberately image-free

No exact reusable image was verified for Harunire Terrace, Tombo-no-Yu's exterior, Picchio/Wild Bird Forest visitor centre, Karuizawa Kogen Church, Stone Church, the Usui observation platform or Karuizawa Station. Those cards are intentionally image-free rather than using an official website photograph without a reuse basis.

`footbath_hoshino.jpg` and `forest_official_hoshino.jpg` remain gallery-only area-mood material with reuse basis unresolved; they are not assigned to a distinct attraction card.

## Safety / live conditions

The planner links the official [Hoshino Area](https://www.hoshino-area.jp/en/) site for live notices. The current planner wording says that bear sightings were reported between Hoshino and Sengataki in June 2026 and instructs visitors to check current notices before forest walks. It does not infer that a sighting means the area is closed or unsafe.
